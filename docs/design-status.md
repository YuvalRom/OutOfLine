# Design status

Current checkpoint: the owner requested saving this version to the repository.
The next planned artwork review is the teacup and bookshelf.

## Intent

Target ages: 4-10. A page should offer an ordinary object in its line drawing
and another subject through its colour regions. The goal is creative thinking:
children can follow the numbers, colour freely, or invent their own reading.

Retain only lines that contribute to the starting or revealed image. Thin
lines are decoration children can colour across; thick lines delimit regions.
All seven pages have seven colour numbers. Example palettes are suggestions.

## Cover and copy

The paper-airplane design is selected **for now**. The cover must not reveal a
puzzle or its answer. Current text, verbatim:

- OutOfLine
- The lines are not the boss of me!
- Go on. Get carried away.
- A colouring book for curious minds
- YOUR IMAGINATION STARTS HERE

Use British spelling of "colour" in the book. Keep the tone playful and direct.
The existing interior instructions are part of the review edition; the cover's
first-person wording does not imply an automatic rewrite of every interior line.
